## IBM Interview Process

### Typical Interview Stages
1. **Online Application** → ATS screening
2. **Recruiter Phone Screen** (30 min): Experience, salary, role fit
3. **Technical Assessment**: Coding test or case study (role-dependent)
4. **Technical Interview** (1-2 rounds): Deep technical assessment
5. **Behavioral Interview**: IBM Values-based questions using STAR
6. **Manager/Panel Interview**: Cross-functional fit assessment
7. **HR Final Round**: Offer discussion and background check

### IBM Values-Based Interview Questions
IBM interviewers specifically probe for alignment with IBM values:

**Trust & Responsibility:**
- "Describe a time when you had to make an unpopular but ethically correct decision."
- "Tell me about a time you took responsibility for a mistake that was partially someone else's fault."

**Innovation:**
- "What is the most innovative solution you've ever built? How did the idea come to you?"
- "Describe a time you challenged the status quo and introduced a new way of working."

**Client Success:**
- "Tell me about a time a client was unsatisfied. What did you do?"
- "Describe a situation where you went significantly beyond expectations for a client."

### IBM-Specific Preparation Tips
1. Research IBM's current earnings report and strategic priorities
2. Study IBM Think conference keynotes for technology direction
3. Know Red Hat's role in IBM's hybrid cloud strategy
4. Familiarize yourself with IBM's AI ethics principles
5. Prepare examples of cross-cultural or remote collaboration
