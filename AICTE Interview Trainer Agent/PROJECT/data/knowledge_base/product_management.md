# Product Management Interview Guide

## Core PM Competency Areas

### Product Thinking
- User empathy and customer research techniques (interviews, surveys, usability tests)
- Defining user personas and customer journey mapping
- Problem discovery vs solution definition
- Jobs-to-be-done (JTBD) framework
- Opportunity sizing and market analysis

### Prioritization Frameworks
| Framework | When to Use |
|---|---|
| RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) | Feature backlog prioritization |
| MoSCoW (Must/Should/Could/Won't) | Release scoping |
| Kano Model | Delighters vs must-haves |
| ICE Score (Impact, Confidence, Ease) | Quick startup decisions |
| Value vs Effort Matrix | Visual prioritization with stakeholders |

### Product Metrics & Analytics
- North Star Metric definition
- AARRR Funnel: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral
- DAU/MAU, Session Duration, Churn Rate, LTV, CAC
- Cohort analysis and funnel drop-off analysis
- A/B testing: hypothesis formation, statistical significance, learning documentation
- NPS (Net Promoter Score) interpretation

### Roadmap & Strategy
- Outcome-based vs output-based roadmaps
- OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework
- Quarterly planning and stakeholder alignment
- Technical debt communication to non-technical leadership
- Go-to-market strategy collaboration with sales/marketing

### Stakeholder Management
- Cross-functional alignment (Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales, Legal)
- Managing up: communicating with C-suite
- Negotiating feature tradeoffs with engineering
- Customer escalations and feedback loops

## Common Interview Questions — Product Manager

### Product Sense Questions
1. "How would you improve IBM Watson's developer experience?"
2. "Design a product for elderly users who want to video call family members."
3. "What metric would you use as the North Star for Spotify? Justify your choice."
4. "How would you decide whether to build Feature A (requested by 1 big customer) vs Feature B (requested by 50 small customers)?"
5. "Walk me through a product you love. What would you change about it?"

### Analytical Questions
6. "Our activation rate dropped 20% after last week's release. How do you diagnose this?"
7. "How would you A/B test a new onboarding flow? What would you measure?"
8. "What does success look like for this product in 12 months? Define the metrics."

### Execution Questions
9. "Walk me through how you'd spec a feature from inception to launch."
10. "An engineer tells you a feature you need will take 3 months. You promised it in 6 weeks. What do you do?"
11. "Describe a product you shipped. What went wrong and how did you handle it?"

### Strategic Questions
12. "How would you build a product strategy for entering a new market segment?"
13. "A competitor just launched a feature your users have been requesting. How do you respond?"

## Experience Level Expectations
- **Associate PM**: User stories, backlog management, stakeholder communication
- **PM (2-4 yrs)**: Feature ownership, data-driven decisions, roadmap contribution
- **Senior PM (5-7 yrs)**: Full product ownership, cross-functional leadership, strategy input
- **Principal/Director**: Product vision, portfolio management, team leadership, exec communication
